This study has two diets (`HC_LF` = high carb, low fat; `HF_LC` = high
fat, low carb) with 9 measurement sets collected on 192 mice:

     Name         Description                 
     Physio       In Vivo Physiological traits
     LivRna       Liver gene expression       
     LivMet       Liver metabolites           
     Enrich       Plasma enrichment over time 
     Lipid        Liver Lipids                
     PlaEnrich    Plasma Time Points          
     LivEnrich    Liver Enrichment            
     PlaMet       Plasma Metabolites          
     LivIso       Liver Isoforms
